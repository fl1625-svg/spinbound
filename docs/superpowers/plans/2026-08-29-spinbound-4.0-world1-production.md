# SPINBOUND 4.0 World 1 Production Slice Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce the complete World 1 vertical-slice content with authored hook-driven stages, one Skill Trial, one large set-piece proof, final-quality representative Rotor/environment assets, and production visual/VFX treatment.

**Architecture:** Gameplay definitions are authored and solver-validated first. Unity scenes are generated/assembled from those definitions, then presentation meshes, shaders, lighting, vegetation, water, VFX, and occlusion-safe decoration are layered on top. No presentation asset is allowed to redefine collision truth.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6000.3.18f1, URP 17.3.0, SRP Batcher, GPU Instancing, baked GI, Light/Reflection Probes, ShaderLab/HLSL, authored mesh assets plus controlled procedural placement.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-spinbound-4.0-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- World 1 visual theme: Daisy Meadow — bright highland garden, flowers, soft cliffs, waterways, playful garden structures.
- World 1 gameplay language: fundamentals, phase reading, L/S bends, speed selection.
- Every normal stage has one hook understandable within about 10 seconds.
- Every stage passes Introduce/Develop/Twist/Finale where appropriate and has at least one zero-damage route.
- Default bird's-eye gameplay view must remain clearer than the lower cinematic view.
- Rotor is highest visual priority; gameplay geometry/hazards second; decoration never competes.
- No asset-store collage and no prototype procedural geometry accepted as final-quality hero/environment reference.
